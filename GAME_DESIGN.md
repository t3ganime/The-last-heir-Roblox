# Game Design — The Last Heir

## High concept
A third-person Roblox adventure RPG set in a fictional world inspired by the diversity of African landscapes, architecture, music, clothing, storytelling, and community life.

## Narrative premise
The protagonist's peaceful village is attacked by armed raiders. The family heirloom—a sword carrying generations of history—is stolen. The boy survives and begins a journey to find the raiders and reclaim the sword. The journey introduces allies, reveals the sword's past, and challenges the hero's belief that revenge will heal his grief.

## Themes
- Revenge versus justice
- Friendship and trust
- Family, memory, and belonging
- Responsibility and forgiveness
- Courage without cruelty

## Player experience
Players should feel curious while exploring, connected to companions, and responsible for important choices. Conflict should serve the story rather than glorify cruelty.

## World regions (working names)
1. **Oru Village:** the hero's home and the emotional starting point.
2. **The Red-Earth Road:** farms, streams, trading posts, and travellers.
3. **The Whispering Forest:** environmental puzzles and a companion quest.
4. **Kaya Market City:** merchants, musicians, craftspeople, and rumours.
5. **The Old Kingdom:** archives and ruins that reveal the sword's history.

Names are placeholders. Review names, language, clothing, architecture, and cultural references with appropriate research.

## Main characters
- **The Heir:** determined, grieving, and initially convinced revenge is the only answer.
- **The Guide:** a practical traveller who teaches the hero to look beyond first impressions.
- **The Inventor:** a clever young companion who solves traversal and puzzle challenges.
- **The Healer:** a compassionate character whose quest explores rebuilding after loss.
- **The Rival:** someone connected to the raiders whose motives are more complicated than they first appear.

## Core gameplay loop
1. Explore a region.
2. Speak with characters and gather clues.
3. Complete a quest, puzzle, or traversal challenge.
4. Earn trust, knowledge, or an item.
5. Make a story choice.
6. Unlock the next region and companion story.

## Narrative choice principle
Choices should have understandable consequences, not a simplistic good/bad score. A revenge-focused response may create short-term access but damage trust; a patient response may reveal information or open a peaceful solution.

## Visual and audio direction
Use a colourful, stylised look with warm natural lighting and distinct regional environments. Build an original soundtrack that draws respectfully from researched musical influences; do not treat Africa as one uniform culture.

## Accessibility and safety
- Clear quest objectives and readable UI.
- Optional subtitles and adjustable audio.
- Avoid graphic depictions of the village attack.
- Keep player interactions moderated and age-appropriate.
